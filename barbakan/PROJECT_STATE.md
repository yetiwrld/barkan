# 2. PROJECT VISION

Build a premium, business-specific digital experience for Barbakan that feels intentionally designed by a professional product/design team.

The project consists of two connected but distinct experiences:

### A. PUBLIC WEBSITE

The premium brand/marketing website used to:

- Introduce Barbakan
- Communicate its identity
- Showcase products
- Showcase bakery/deli offerings
- Build trust
- Provide useful business information
- Help customers discover the physical business
- Direct customers toward ordering where appropriate

### B. ORDERING PLATFORM

A dedicated customer-facing ordering experience that can be shared directly through a link.

Customers should eventually be able to:

- Create an account
- Log in
- Browse products
- View categories
- View product details
- Add products to cart
- Change quantities
- Checkout
- Submit orders
- Receive confirmation
- View previous orders where implemented
- Manage their account
- Log out

The ordering platform must feel like a premium extension of the Barbakan brand rather than a generic ecommerce template.
